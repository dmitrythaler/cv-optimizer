Job description
Senior Fullstack Engineer (React & Node.js)

About the Role
We are looking for a Senior Fullstack Engineer to join a product team building modern, scalable web applications and digital experiences.
You will work across the entire application stack, from user-facing interfaces to backend services and integrations. The role has a strong focus on frontend engineering while also involving backend development, API design, and data management.
You will collaborate closely with product, design, and engineering teams to deliver high-quality, accessible, and performant applications.

Responsibilities
Develop and maintain modern web applications using React, Node.js, and TypeScript.

Build scalable and reusable UI components integrated with a design system.

Develop and maintain backend services and APIs using Node.js and Fastify.

Integrate applications with CMS platforms and third-party services.

Work with PostgreSQL and Redis to support application functionality and performance.

Write and maintain automated tests, including unit and end-to-end tests.

Ensure high standards of accessibility, performance, and code quality.

Participate in architectural discussions and contribute to engineering best practices.


Requirements
Strong commercial experience in fullstack development using React and Node.js.

Solid experience with modern frontend technologies, including React Router and TanStack Query.

Experience integrating with Headless CMS platforms, preferably Contentful.

Good understanding of API design and backend development principles.

Experience with PostgreSQL and Redis.

Experience with automated testing frameworks such as Vitest and Playwright.

Strong understanding of accessibility standards (WCAG) and performance optimisation.

Ability to work across the entire software development lifecycle in a product-oriented environment.


Nice to Have
Experience with authentication and identity platforms such as SAP Gigya.

Experience integrating Salesforce products or loyalty platforms.

Experience in highly scalable, customer-facing digital products.

Experience working in agile, cross-functional product teams.
